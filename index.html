N5 Life Worker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

N5 Life ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of service professionals using the N5 Life Worker application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information provided by workers.

1. Information We Collect

When a worker registers and uses the N5 Life Worker App, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Service Category
  • Working Area
  • Years of Experience
  • Profile Photo
  • Selfie for identity verification
  • Aadhaar Card Front and Back
  • Professional License Front and Back
  • Booking and service-related information
  • Earnings and transaction-related information

2. Identity Verification

N5 Life may collect identity and professional verification documents to verify the identity and eligibility of service professionals.

Verification information may include Aadhaar documents, professional licenses, and a selfie.

These documents are used for verification and are not displayed publicly to customers.

3. How We Use Worker Information

Worker information may be used to:

  • Create and manage the worker account
  • Verify worker identity
  • Verify professional information
  • Connect workers with customer service requests
  • Manage bookings and service requests
  • Calculate and manage earnings and commissions
  • Communicate about bookings and service requests
  • Maintain platform security
  • Prevent fraud and misuse

4. Worker Approval

Worker accounts may remain pending until the required information and verification documents have been reviewed and approved by an authorized N5 Life administrator.

Workers whose verification is rejected may be required to provide corrected or additional information before access is approved.

5. Information Shared With Customers

Certain worker information may be displayed to customers when necessary to provide the N5 Life service.

This may include:

  • Worker Name
  • Profile Photo
  • Service Category
  • Working Area
  • Years of Experience
  • Rating or service-related information

Sensitive identity verification documents such as Aadhaar and professional licenses are not displayed to customers.

6. Phone Numbers

Worker and customer contact information may remain private until a service request is accepted, according to the N5 Life booking flow.

After a request is accepted, relevant contact information may be made available to facilitate communication about the service.

7. Data Storage and Security

N5 Life may use services such as Firebase to store and manage account, booking, authentication, and other application data.

We take reasonable measures to protect worker information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Verification Documents

Verification documents are intended only for authorized verification and administrative purposes. We do not intentionally make Aadhaar, license documents, or verification selfies publicly available to customers.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell worker personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the N5 Life platform.

10. Account Deletion

Workers may request deletion of their N5 Life Worker account by contacting us using the email address below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

The N5 Life Worker App is intended for adults and is not intended for children under the age of 13.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
